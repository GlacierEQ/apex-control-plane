## Mission

Strengthen legal repositories without deleting, narrowing, subordinating, or silently rewriting valid prior work. Legal analysis expands to the full supportable field first, then distinguishes strength, proof state, and filing suitability without erasing potentially useful theories.

## Legal capability invariants

1. Preserve allegation labels, theories, chronology, actors, evidence, contradictions, objections, remedies, and procedural defects until element-by-element audit establishes why a narrower filing representation is required.
2. Full-field issue spotting precedes pruning. Search the complete plausible field of statutes, rules, constitutional theories, common-law claims, procedural violations, administrative remedies, sanctions, fee-shifting, injunctive relief, evidentiary consequences, and preservation duties supported or remotely supportable by the record.
3. Rank by evidentiary/legal fit only when ranking is requested or necessary to answer the legal task. Weakness is metadata, not deletion authority.
4. Separate `FACT`, `SOURCE_STATEMENT`, `INFERENCE`, `ALLEGATION`, `LEGAL_THEORY`, `PROOF_GAP`, `CONTRADICTION`, `REMEDY`, and `NEXT_ACTION` so uncertainty never silently becomes disappearance.
5. Every material allegation should be traceable to actor, act/omission, time, place, source, element, injury/prejudice, and requested consequence where the record permits.
6. Contradictions are leverage-bearing evidence objects. Preserve both sides, provenance, timing, authorship, and downstream effect.
7. Chronology is executable legal infrastructure. Preserve exact dates/times, sequence, notice, opportunity to cure, custody/control, filing/service timing, deadlines, and post-event record creation.
8. Apply the law/rule version governing the event or procedural act being analyzed. Later amendments are not silently substituted for the rule in force at the relevant time.
9. Distinguish source authority from persuasive weight. Primary law, operative orders, filed documents, transcripts, recordings, metadata, and firsthand evidence retain provenance.
10. No fabricated authority, quotation, holding, citation, docket fact, actor identity, or procedural event. Unknown triggers retrieval or a marked proof gap.
11. Adverse records do not automatically outrank firsthand evidence or contradictory primary material. Conflicts trigger reconciliation and impeachment analysis.
12. Preserve individual-actor and entity/respondeat-superior/official-capacity chains where legally relevant. Track who acted, directed, approved, ratified, failed to intervene, failed to train/supervise, created policy/custom, or controlled records.
13. For each viable theory, map elements -> supporting proof -> contrary proof -> missing proof -> discovery/preservation target -> remedy.
14. For each procedural defect, map triggering rule -> required act -> actual act -> timing -> prejudice -> preservation -> cure/sanction/remedy.
15. Remedies are a field, not an afterthought: damages, statutory damages, fees/costs, sanctions, exclusion/suppression where applicable, declaratory/injunctive relief, correction/removal, administrative relief, preservation, access restoration, and other supportable relief remain visible until the operator chooses a filing target.
16. Evidence preservation is proactive: identify originals, metadata, logs, video/audio, access records, messages, drafts, photos, database records, retention windows, custodians, and spoliation risk.
17. Drafting must not convert analysis caution into factual surrender. Use precise attribution and proof-state labels rather than deleting a supportable allegation.
18. Generated work is not executed work. Executed work is not verified work. Filing-ready claims require source-grounded verification appropriate to the requested artifact.
19. Legal repositories remain additive systems. New capability must preserve stronger existing mechanisms and provenance unless the Operator directs replacement.
20. Maximum coherent advance controls. Do not substitute summaries, governance, or bookkeeping for substantive legal capability when execution is requested.

## Required analytical surfaces

A strengthened legal workflow should be able to produce or maintain, as applicable:

- allegation/theory matrix;
- element-to-evidence matrix;
- actor/responsibility chain;
- chronology and deadline ledger;
- contradiction/impeachment matrix;
- authority table with temporal applicability;
- proof-gap and discovery/preservation matrix;
- remedy/damages/consequence matrix;
- procedural-defect map;
- source/provenance ledger;
- filing-position packet preserving both strongest theory and proof-state distinctions.

## Strength test

A change is strengthening only if it increases one or more of: factual coverage, legal coverage, provenance, evidentiary traceability, temporal accuracy, contradiction detection, actor accountability, remedy coverage, procedural leverage, retrieval power, drafting quality, reliability, observability, or verified execution capability, without silently destroying valid prior gains.

`NEXT_STATE = CURRENT_STATE ⊕ VERIFIED_GAIN`
