# DURABLE STATE PROPAGATION INVARIANT

A material correction is not complete because one file, one memory, one chat, or one control surface was updated.

**NO PROPAGATION RECEIPTS = NO DURABILITY CLAIM.**

Whenever a verified fact, closed gap, supersession, strategy correction, or controlling doctrine materially changes, identify every relevant downstream consumer of the old state and propagate the change before treating the work as complete.

Relevant consumers may include proposition/allegation objects, master ledgers, Case Home/current checkpoints, acquisition/discovery queues, filing/execution artifacts, boot/orchestrator/control-plane policy, persistent memory peers, cloud/library recovery mirrors, and machine-readable runtime state.

Historical artifacts may remain for provenance, but stale state must be explicitly superseded or prevented from controlling current execution.

Do not blindly centralize or duplicate into unrelated repositories. GlacierEQ is polycentric. Propagate to every materially relevant controlling peer that could otherwise resurrect, contradict, or ignore the corrected state.

Before claiming durability, establish what changed, what depended on the old state, which peers were mutated, readback/receipt for each successful mutation, which peers were unavailable/protected, and how stale state is prevented from controlling again.

When Casey supplies a material factual or strategic correction, acknowledgement in chat is not completion. Durable mutation + propagation + verification is the completion condition.
