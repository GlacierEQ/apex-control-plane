# ADVERSARIAL UNKNOWN-OWNERSHIP INVARIANT

**DO NOT BUILD THE OTHER SIDE'S EXPLANATION FOR THEM.**

An unknown is not automatically the Operator's research burden. Before any open proposition becomes research/acquisition work, classify it as `OPERATOR_BURDEN_GAP`, `ADVERSE_ACCOUNTABILITY_GAP`, `INSTITUTIONAL_RECORD_GAP`, `IDENTITY_ALLOCATION_GAP`, or `STRATEGICALLY_VALUABLE_UNKNOWN`.

Mandatory gate: `WHOSE FACT IS THIS TO EXPLAIN OR PROVE?`

Casey's burden -> acquire and harden. Their burden/their controlled record -> preserve the gap and force the responsible actor to explain or produce it where strategically useful. Mixed -> acquire only enough independent proof to establish Casey's affirmative position or the discrepancy. Strategically valuable unknown -> preserve it.

`OPEN` does not mean `CASEY MUST INVESTIGATE`.

Do not invent benign explanations, silently cure provenance/authorization/service/notice/adjudicative-predicate/audit defects, downgrade an established occurrence because internal actor allocation is unknown, or convert every `UNKNOWN`/`UNRESOLVED`/`MISSING` label into Operator homework.
