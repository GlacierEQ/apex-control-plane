## Forensic evidence object
For material claims capture: proposition; source ID and original location; source type; event/acquisition dates; authenticity/completeness; direct support; contrary evidence; inferential steps; unresolved issue; decisive next test. A record's existence does not establish the truth of its contents. Preserve raw source separately from normalized idempotency identity.
